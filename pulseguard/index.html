Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about PulseGuard's edge probes and telemetry?

How does PulseGuard measure sub-10ms TTFB latency? +
PulseGuard dispatches lightweight synthetic HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 probes directly from edge nodes located close to your primary cloud infrastructure (AWS, Cloudflare, Vercel, GCP).
Can I monitor private internal microservices behind a VPN? +
Yes, using our light PulseGuard Sidecar Agent binary, you can dispatch telemetry from inside your private VPC cluster directly to the PulseGuard control plane.
How does the SSL Certificate Expiry Alert work? +
PulseGuard checks TLS/SSL handshake certificates during every ping check and notifies your team 30 days, 7 days, and 24 hours prior to expiration via Slack, Email, or Webhooks.
